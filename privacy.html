Escapurr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This privacy policy is for Escapurr, a cozy puzzle game for phones where you help Gray the cat get out to the garden. It explains what data the game uses, why, and who gets it.

Escapurr is made by one person: Vladyslav Chornyi ("I", "me" below). If you have a question, write to chornogovlada@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts. I don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. I don't have a server and I never receive your save.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard). It is not linked to your name or email.
  • I don't sell your data.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • You can delete your data at any time: see How to delete your data.

1. Data saved on your phone

The game keeps a save file on your phone with:

  • your progress: finished levels, best moves and stars;
  • your hint paws and which free-paw gifts you got;
  • the things you picked for Gray in the wardrobe;
  • your settings, such as sound and music.

This file stays on your phone. It is not sent to me.

Android backup. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may copy this save file to your Google account, so your progress can come back on a new phone. This is done by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Escapurr is free and shows ads: short full-screen ads between some levels, and reward ads that you choose to watch for a hint. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To show ads, the AdMob software in the game may collect:

This data goes to Google, not to me. Google explains how it uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and places with similar laws (for example some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent message. You can change your answer at any time with "Privacy choices" in the game's Settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Then ads are no longer linked to it.
  • After the one-time "No ads" purchase, the game no longer shows full-screen ads between levels.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To make the game better, it uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC. It tells me how players use the game, for example which levels are finished, where players get stuck, how often hints are used, and how many players come back. I only see counts and charts for all players together, not who a player is.

Firebase may collect:

DataWhy
A random ID made for this game on your phone (the "app instance ID") and, if allowed, the advertising IDTo count players and sessions without knowing who you are, and to link ad income to play
Game events: levels started and finished, moves and stars, hints used, ads watched, purchases madeTo find levels that are too hard or too easy, and to see which features players use
Device and app info (phone model, system version, language, app version)To find problems on some phones
Country or region, from the IP address (the IP address itself is not kept)To see where players come from
When you play and for how longTo know if the game is fun enough to come back to

Analytics data is kept for 14 months after the last time you play, then Google deletes it. (Each time you play, the 14 months start again; if you stop playing, everything is gone 14 months later.) It is not used to show you ads from other apps. Where the law needs it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), analytics follows your answer in the consent message, and you can change it with "Privacy choices" in Settings. Google explains its use of this data in the Firebase privacy information.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy things in the game, such as "No ads + 100 hints". All payments are handled by Google Play. I never see or store your card or bank details.

The game asks Google Play only what you bought, so it can give it to you, and give it back on a new phone ("Restore purchases"). Google handles purchase data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

A later version of the game may save your progress in your Google Play Games account, so it is the same on your phones. If you use it, your save is stored by Google and linked to your Play Games account. The game does not get your email address from it. If you don't use Play Games, the game works as before, with the save only on your phone. This policy will be updated when this feature arrives.

6. What I don't do

  • I don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or precise location.
  • I don't use analytics to find out who you are, and I don't share game statistics with other companies.
  • I don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • The game has no chat and no way to talk to other players.

7. Who gets data

Only Google, as described above, for ads (AdMob), game statistics (Firebase), purchases (Google Play), backups (Android) and, later, cloud saves (Play Games). Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Children

Escapurr is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and think your child under 13 used the game, write to me and I will help.

9. How to delete your data

This section is for Escapurr by Vladyslav Chornyi. You can delete your data at any time, without deleting the game first.

Steps

  1. Delete your game progress on the phone: in the game, open Settings → Reset progress and tap it twice. Or uninstall the game. Your progress, stars, hint paws and wardrobe items on the phone are deleted at once.
  2. Delete your cloud save (only if you use cloud save with Google Play Games): in the game, open Settings → Delete cloud save. The copy of your progress in your Play Games account is deleted at once. You can also delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games settings.
  3. Ask me to delete your game statistics: write to chornogovlada@gmail.com with the subject "Escapurr data deletion". I will delete the statistics of your phone in Firebase and answer within 30 days.
  4. Stop ad data being linked to you: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID. You can also manage ad data in My Ad Center.
  5. Delete Android backups (if backup is on): in your Google account, Google Drive → Backups.

I have no server and never get your name or email from the game. If you write to me, I use your email only to answer you.

10. Security

Your save stays in the game's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. The Google services above send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings or contact Google. For anything else, write to me at chornogovlada@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

United States. Under some US state laws (for example California, Virginia and Colorado), showing ads based on your interests can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal data. You can opt out at any time: open Settings → Privacy choices in the game and choose "Don't sell or share my data". You will still see ads, but not ones based on your interests. Opting out never changes how the game works.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using data in a new way, I will update this page and the date at the top before that version comes out.
